Secret Message

Dear Mommy,

Happy Mother's Day! I feel really bad that I didn't make it home this weekend but I hope you know I was thinking about you the entire time.

I'm so grateful for all the values and lessons you've instilled in me. And I don't say this enough but you've always been someone I've looked up to as a role model for doing it all. You balance being a mom, having a successful career, showing up for your friends, students, teachers, parents, etc., all while protecting your passions for cooking, gardening, and leading our family. It's inspired me to never have to be one thing either. In high school, you inspired me to balance school with sports. But since coming to college, I've actualized so many more of your lessons that I'd subconsciously picked up from your strength over the years: how to be strong but still kind and true-to-myself in male spaces, the elegance of kindness, the importance of the people around me and their influence, the power of being the bigger person even when it feels like you're being taken advantage of, the comfort of a clean home to come back to (who knew), the strength of a loving support system, and so many more that I probably just haven't realized yet.

In fact, this time apart has made me excited to come back home for the summer and have home-cooked meals again (automatic request for gyros please!!) and movie nights that you and I will probably fall asleep to ;)

I love you so much and I hope you had an amazing Mother's Day!

Love,
Ash
